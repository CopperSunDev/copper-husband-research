# Relationship Research — Husband Field Guide

Husband Field Guide curates the research behind a strong marriage — the longitudinal studies, experiments, and meta-analyses on what helps partners connect, handle conflict, and stay satisfied — and annotates each source with what it actually found, including where the evidence is mixed or modest. It reports research and recommends nothing; the app itself is a private memory and connection tool, not counseling or therapy.

> **Educational information, not professional advice.** This is a curated summary of published research for general information. It is not counseling, therapy, medical, or legal advice, and not a recommendation for your relationship. Research describes averages, not individuals — consider a qualified professional for your own situation. Husband Field Guide and Copper Sun Content and Creative, LLC are not counselors or therapists; individual results vary.

## How this index is built

- **Primary sources only**, each verified by fetching it — it resolves and is **not retracted**. No source is written from memory.
- **Neutral:** every entry reports what a source found, attributed, with its limitations. It recommends nothing and draws no conclusions.
- **Balanced on purpose:** null, mixed, and adverse findings are included, not just the favorable ones.
- **Last verified:** November 2026.
- **Generated from a single canonical config** — this repo is a derived mirror; it is not hand-edited.
